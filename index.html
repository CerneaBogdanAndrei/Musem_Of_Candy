ABOUT SWEET DREAMS

Sweet Dreams Museum is a playful candy themed experience where every corner looks like a photo from your favorite dessert shop. Walk through giant donuts, glowing gummy bears and clouds of cotton candy while music, lights and colors turn each room into a different world. Bring your friends, your camera and your sweet tooth.

TICKETS AND VISIT INFO

Tickets include access to all interactive rooms, candy tasting stops and a small gift at the exit. Choose the time slot that works for you, check in with your digital ticket and enjoy a one hour tour full of color and sugar. Group reservations, school visits and birthday packages are available on request by email.

WHY YOU WILL LOVE IT

Take colorful photos, taste limited edition sweets and relax in cozy lounge corners filled with pillows shaped like candy. Every visit is different, because we change themes and installations during the year. Whether you come with friends, family or on a date, Sweet Dreams Museum is designed to make you smile and forget about your day for a while.
